OFFICIAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) DOC ID: TF-MANUAL-2026 · REV 2.4

TodoFlow Workspace Operations Manual

Official Standard Operating Guidelines for Task Delivery, Milestone Velocity, Time Tracking & Team Collaboration

Target Audience: Employees & Managers Effective Date: October 2026 Issuing Authority: Workspace Administration Status: Active Document

Chapter 1: Workspace Architecture & System Navigation

TodoFlow is designed to help team members organize deliverables, meet operational deadlines, and maintain full transparency with management. The primary navigation is located in the left sidebar:

  • Dashboard: Displays an executive summary of your active tasks, completion statistics, trend charts, and urgent notifications.
  • My Tasks: A centralized workspace listing all assignments with filtering by priority, deadline, status tabs, and tags.
  • Activity Log: A chronological timeline recording every task modification, milestone reached, and comment posted.
  • Reports & Analytics: Visual performance metrics, velocity graphs, and individual/combined employee printable reports.
  • Conversations: Real-time messaging and operational communication between employees and admin.
  • About & Guide: Complete documentation and keyboard shortcuts reference.

Chapter 2: The 4-Stage Task Execution Velocity

Every assignment follows four distinct execution milestones so your progress is instantly visible to the team without requiring manual status meetings:

  • Stage 1 — Pending / Preparing: Selected when a task is assigned or when reviewing instructions, gathering assets, or setting up prerequisites.
  • Stage 2 — Started: Activated as soon as hands-on execution has officially commenced.
  • Stage 3 — Mid Progress: Chosen once the majority of the work is completed and final refinements are underway.
  • Stage 4 — Finished: Marked when all objectives have been accomplished, logging your exact completion timestamp and updating team velocity.

Chapter 3: Live Daily Work Status & Attendance

Team members can broadcast their real-time working status directly from the header banner to maintain active communication:

  • Start: Signals that you have commenced your shift and are actively working on assignments.
  • Preparing: Indicates you are setting up your workspace or reviewing incoming requests.
  • Finish: Signals that you have concluded your primary tasks for the day.
  • Exit: Indicates that you have completed your shift and logged off.
  • Status Notes: You can attach an optional note to provide specific context regarding your current focus.

Chapter 4: Deadlines, Timers & Urgent Alert Banners

Tasks assigned with explicit deadlines feature automated countdown tracking to help you prioritize urgent duties:

  • Top Persistent Banner: When you have an active task, a dedicated alert banner remains visible on your screen until the task is finished.
  • Warning Stage: When approaching your deadline, the banner highlights your remaining minutes in an alert color.
  • Dead Time Alert: During the final critical minutes before deadline expiration, the banner transitions into an urgent red indicator reminding you to finish immediately.

Chapter 5: Subtask Checklists & Live Stopwatch

Complex projects can be broken down into structured steps for methodical execution:

  • Checklist Subtasks: Check off individual subtasks inside the task details modal as you complete them to track gradual completion.
  • Built-In Stopwatch: Click the stopwatch icon on any task card to start a live timer that logs your actual working hours directly to the task.

Chapter 6: Comments, Attachments & Profile Security

Effective collaboration and account management are facilitated through integrated profile tools:

  • Task Comments: Leave notes, updates, or questions inside any task to receive direct feedback from the administrator.
  • Profile Photo (DP): Upload your profile image from the profile modal; it is automatically optimized to display cleanly across all devices.
  • Direct Email Updates: Change your email address directly from your profile settings anytime.
  • Password Management: Update your account password at any time by entering your current password followed by your new password.
